A and G, built as robot arms

The two initials are the Joint arm: links, pin joints, a piston, apricot end effectors. The rest of the name is Rethink Sans 800, and the arm links match its stroke weight, so the name still reads as one word. The A stands up from its base plate and the G curls in joint by joint; shish and upta stay still. Click any stage to replay.
