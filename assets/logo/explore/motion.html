Five ways for the arms to move

Same letters as round 3, new motion. Every joint now runs on a damped spring, so links overshoot and settle like real hardware, and the apricot tip taps or pings when it lands. Each card pairs an A motion with a G motion that suits it. Mix any two at the bottom. Click a stage to replay it.
